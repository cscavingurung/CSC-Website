FAQ

Questions clients ask before they call.

If yours isn't here, ask us directly on the free consultation — no charge either way.

How much do your services cost?

Fixed fees per service, quoted upfront before you commit to anything — never a percentage of your application or a surprise add-on later.

How long does the process actually take?

It depends on the visa category and IRCC's current processing times, which we're upfront about — not just our own paperwork. We'll give you a realistic range in your first consultation, not a marketing number.

Are you a licensed immigration consultant?

Yes — we're a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant (RCIC), which means we're authorized to represent clients directly with IRCC and are bound by a professional code of conduct.

What happens if my application gets refused?

We review the refusal letter with you, explain exactly what went wrong, and tell you honestly whether it's fixable — before you pay us to try again.

Can you help me sponsor my parents or spouse from Nepal?

Yes — spousal, parent, and dependent-child sponsorship is one of the most common things we handle. We'll give you an honest read on current processing backlogs before you apply.

How do I get started?

Book a free 30-minute consultation in Nepali or English. We'll map your options before you spend anything, so you know exactly what you're signing up for.
