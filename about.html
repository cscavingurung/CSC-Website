ABOUT US

Built for Nepalese who are already here.

Canadian Study Center is a regulated immigration consultancy serving Nepalis living across Canada. We started it because too many people were getting patchy advice on their status, PR, or sponsoring family back home — we wanted to do it properly, and be honest about what's actually possible.

OUR STORY

Why we started this.

Canadian Study Center was founded by a small group of consultants who had already been through the process ourselves — as students who became permanent residents, as people who sponsored our own parents from Nepal, and as people who watched friends get burned by agents promising guarantees no one can honestly make.

We built this consultancy around one idea: tell clients the truth about their chances, their timeline, and their costs before they pay us anything. That's meant turning away applicants whose profile wasn't ready yet, and it's meant harder conversations up front — but it's also why our approval rate holds up.

Today we work with Nepalis living across Canada — on study permit extensions, work permits, permanent residence, and sponsoring family from Nepal — each with a named consultant who stays on the file from the first call through to approval.
