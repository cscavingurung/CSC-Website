HOW IT WORKS

Five stages, one consultant the whole way.

You'll always know which stage your file is in and who to ask. Here's exactly what happens at each step, and what we need from you.

1

Free consultation

30 minutes, in Nepali or English, to understand your situation and map out realistic options — before you spend anything.

What we cover

Your current status, goals, and which pathway actually fits.

What you bring

Nothing formal — just be ready to talk through your situation.

2

Document checklist

A single shared checklist built for your specific case — upload documents straight from your phone as you gather them.

What we cover

Exactly which documents your case needs, and why.

What you bring

IDs, current permits, and any supporting evidence we request.

3

Filing

We prepare and file your application directly with IRCC, and give you the exact application or receipt number to track.

What we cover

Final review, submission, and confirmation of receipt.

What you bring

Sign-off on the final application before it's submitted.

4

Tracking

You get status updates as soon as IRCC updates your file — not just when you happen to ask us.

What we cover

Monitoring your file and flagging anything that needs a response.

What you bring

Quick turnaround if IRCC requests further evidence.

5

Approval & next steps

Once you're approved, we help plan what's next — a renewal, your PR pathway, or sponsoring family from Nepal.

What we cover

A short call to plan your next move before this file even closes.

What you bring

Just your goals for what comes after this approval.
